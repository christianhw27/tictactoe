TIC TAC TOE

Endless Strategy

3x3 Mode Turn: X

Max symbols per player: 3

Oldest symbol will be removed when limit is reached.

How to Play

  • Infinite: No draws. If the board is full or limit reached, your first symbol disappears.
  • Winning: Align symbols (Horizontal, Vertical, Diagonal).
  • 3x3: Need 3 in a row (Max 3 symbols).
  • 5x5: Need 4 in a row (Max 4 symbols).
  • 6x6: Need 5 in a row (Max 5 symbols).
